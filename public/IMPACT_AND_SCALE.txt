WeatherGPT — IMPACT, SCALE & COST MODEL (Transparent)
=====================================================
Smart India Hackathon Round-2. Planning & Operational Architecture.

A) PROBLEM & BENEFICIARIES
--------------------------
Primary:
  - Small and marginal farmers: Irrigation, fertilizer & spray scheduling.
  - Village communities & Gram Panchayats: Early warning for floods, cloudbursts, severe thunderstorms.
  - School administrators: Heatwave / outdoor advisories.
  - District Disaster Management Authorities (DDMA): Coordinated multi-channel dispatch.

B) RURAL REACH LAYERS
---------------------
Layer 1 (Smartphone User):
  - Progressive Web App (PWA) with grounded bilingual AI, live NWP models, and voice interface.

Layer 2 (Feature Phone / Low-Connectivity Rural Citizen):
  - 160-Char DLT SMS payload (copy-ready & direct SMS URI).
  - Village IVR Loudspeaker announcement broadcast by Sarpanch / ASHA worker.
  - WhatsApp FPO community forward.

Layer 3 (National Scale Gateway):
  - Institutional bulk push via Government CDAC / NIC SMS Gateway & DLT registration.

C) COST MODEL (INR)
-------------------
  - Web Hosting: Serverless Edge (Vercel / Cloudflare) ≈ ₹0 for pilot scale.
  - Weather APIs: Open-Meteo Open Data ≈ Free tier for moderate QPS.
  - SMS DLT Commercial Rate: ₹0.15 per SMS.

District Pilot Example:
  - 50,000 opted-in farmers.
  - 4 RED Alert emergency events per year.
  - Cost = 50,000 × 4 × ₹0.15 = ₹30,000 / year total SMS budget.
  - Per farmer cost per year = ₹0.60 (paisa level).

D) STAGE ROADMAP TO NATIONAL DEPLOYMENT
---------------------------------------
  - Stage 0 (Current): Live multi-model NWP engine + Grounded AI + Rural Relay Studio + 6 Indic Languages.
  - Stage 1: District Agri University / KVK pilot in 2 blocks with SMS relay.
  - Stage 2: State Department of Agriculture portal embedding & Bhashini API integration.
  - Stage 3: Direct IMD radar & Doppler polygon integration via official MoU.
